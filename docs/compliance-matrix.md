# TheChickenMan: Compliance & Regulatory Requirements Matrix

This matrix maps statutory Indian regulations (FSSAI food safety, GST taxation, electronic invoicing, cold chain transportation, data privacy) to platform workflows and technical controls.

| Regulatory Domain | Authority / Regulation | Specific Requirement | Platform Implementation & Technical Control | Status in MVP |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Food Safety** | Food Safety and Standards Act, 2006 (FSSAI) | Mandatory 14-digit FSSAI license for food business operators (suppliers, slaughterhouses, processors). | Captured during Supplier & Buyer KYC (`fssai_license_number`). Validated format and expiry before Admin approval. | Phase 1 MVP |
| **Traceability** | FSSAI Food Safety and Standards (Licensing & Registration) Regulations | Batch numbering, processing date, and best-before date for perishable poultry meat. | Schema mandates `inventory_batches` (`batch_number`, `production_date`, `expiry_date`, `storage_condition`). Carried forward into `order_items` and invoice snapshots. | Phase 1 MVP |
| **Taxation** | Goods and Services Tax (GST) Act | Mandatory 15-character alphanumeric GSTIN validation for B2B input tax credit. | Captured in `supplier_kyc` and `buyer_kyc`. Required for B2B invoice generation. | Phase 1 MVP |
| **Invoicing** | Central Goods and Services Tax Rules (Rule 46) | Tax invoice must state supplier GSTIN, buyer GSTIN, consecutive serial number, HSN code, tax rate (CGST/SGST or IGST). | `invoices` engine calculates tax based on place of supply (Intrastate: CGST 2.5% + SGST 2.5%; Interstate: IGST 5%). Generates immutable invoice records with tax breakdown. | Phase 1 MVP |
| **E-Way Bill** | GST E-Way Bill System (Rule 138) | Consignment value exceeding ₹50,000 requires e-way bill generation for inter/intrastate movement. | Data model captures vehicle number, dispatch address, and delivery PIN codes to support downstream e-way bill integration. | Phase 1 Data Ready |
| **Weights & Measures**| Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules | Accurate net quantity declaration in standard SI units (kg/grams). | Inventory and order specifications strictly enforce weight in standard kilogram (`NUMERIC(10,2)`). Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ) enforced. | Phase 1 MVP |
| **Proof of Delivery** | Commercial Contract Law / Indian Evidence Act | Verifiable acknowledgment of goods received, shortage reporting, and condition at handover. | Driver captures OTP verification, digital recipient signature, photo of delivered crates, and records accepted vs rejected weight with structured rejection reasons. | Phase 1 MVP |
| **Financial Security** | RBI Payment Aggregator / Gateway Guidelines | Card/Banking details must not be stored in plain text. Webhooks must be verified and idempotent. | Payment provider abstraction delegating sensitive processing to certified gateways. Strict signature verification and webhook idempotency keys. | Phase 1 MVP |
| **Data Privacy** | Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP) | Lawful processing, purpose limitation, and storage security for KYC documents. | Sensitive business documents (PAN, Cheques, GST certificates) stored in controlled storage; accessible only to verified actors via secure endpoints. | Phase 1 MVP |
